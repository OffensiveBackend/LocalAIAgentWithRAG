About Us
----------
Bella Napoli Artisan Pizzeria is a family-owned restaurant established in 2012. Our philosophy is that every pizza should be handcrafted using fresh, locally sourced ingredients whenever possible.

Restaurant Culture
-------------------
Our staff are encouraged to treat every customer like family. We believe in patience, friendliness, and transparency. Every employee undergoes customer-service training before serving guests.
